# Taste in Digital Product Design

Design taste is the disciplined ability to discern between what is merely functional and what is refined, balanced, and timeless.

## The Pillars of Design Taste

1. **Restraint Over Excess**:
   - Resist the urge to add unnecessary glows, neon borders, or gratuitous gradients.
   - Let content and negative space breathe.
   - Use color with deliberate intention (e.g. 90% neutral canvas, 10% purposeful accent).

2. **Typographic Rhythm & Micro-Scale**:
   - Master font size ratios (e.g. 11px micro-labels, 13px body, 15px card titles, 24px headings).
   - Use tabular lining figures (`font-mono` or `font-feature-settings: "tnum"`) for dates, numbers, and scores.
   - Never compromise letter spacing: tighten titles (`tracking-tight`), space uppercase identifiers (`tracking-wider`).

3. **Tactile Depth & Glass Dynamics**:
   - Layer surfaces with subtle border opacity (`border-white/[0.08]`) rather than heavy drop shadows.
   - Soft, diffused backdrops (`backdrop-blur-xl bg-zinc-950/80`).

4. **Information Ergonomics**:
   - Prioritize scannability: users should understand the state of the document in under 3 seconds.
   - Use crisp status badges with harmonious, desaturated background tints (e.g. `bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-400 border-emerald-500/20`).

5. **Eliminating "AI Slop"**:
   - Avoid generic placeholder text or robotic bullet points; write authentic, compelling, industry-grade phrasing.
   - Ensure pixel-perfect baseline alignment across all columns.
